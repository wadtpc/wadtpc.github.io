About Me

A little more than the surface.

Hi, I'm Dorothy 🌷

An aspiring front-end developer and UI/UX designer based in Cebu, Philippines. I'm currently teaching myself full-stack development with Next.js and Supabase, and I love building tools that solve real problems for the communities I'm part of.

My current focus is a management app for our church organization, but I'm also exploring UI/UX design in Figma and helping lead the production team for our 1st Archdiocesan Convention. I believe good tech should feel warm, personal, and useful — not cold or complicated.

🌟
Fun fact: I can't start a project without designing it in my head first — usually at 2 AM.
