About Me

A little more than the surface.

Hi, I'm
Dorothy 🌷

Aspiring front-end developer and UI/UX designer based in Cebu, Philippines, currently building a full-stack church management platform using Next.js and Supabase. Alongside development, I contribute to UI/UX design in Figma and serve as part of the production team for the 1st Archdiocesan Convention. My design philosophy centers on warmth, usability, and community impact.

★ Fun fact: I can't start a project without designing it in my head first — usually at 2 AM.
