CSN de Cebu Web

A full-stack management platform for a multi-chapter religious organization at the Basilica Minore del Santo Niño de Cebu. Handles member registration & login, announcements, member directory, event management (Sinulog, meetings), attendance tracking, payment history, and a Data Privacy Act-compliant consent flow — with role-based dashboards that route admins and members automatically.
